What we do not claim

  • We do not say how many tonnes of CO2 a tree removes.
  • We do not call any purchase an offset, or any company carbon neutral.
  • We do not promise a tree lives for ever. We log the ones that do not.
  • We do say where a tree is, when it was planted, and who planted it.
  • We do say whether it was alive at each check.
  • We do publish the whole register, failures included.

Why: tree-based offset claims are widely criticised, and rules on environmental claims are tightening.

How a tree is checked

  1. Planting day. The planter takes a photo and the phone records the point and its accuracy. Records with poor accuracy are held back.
  2. 3 months. The partner revisits and logs the tree as alive, dead or missing, with a photo.
  3. 12 months. Same check. We also visit a random sample ourselves.
  4. Independent check. A third party reviews a sample each year. Planned, not arranged.
