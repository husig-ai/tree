How it works

  1. Choose

    Pick a project, how many trees, and whether it is a gift.

  2. A local partner plants

    A community forest group plants and looks after the trees. The partner is named on the project page.

  3. We check

    Photos and a GPS point at planting, then again at 3 and 12 months. Failed trees are logged, not hidden.

  4. You follow

    Open your tree page any time. Share it, or print the certificate.

Proof, not promises

We do not tell you how much carbon a tree removes. We show you that the tree exists, where it is, and whether it is still alive.

Questions

Do you sell carbon credits or offsets?

No. This concept sells the planting and care of trees. It does not claim tonnes of CO2 removed and does not tell a company it is carbon neutral. (Copy waits on legal advice.)

How do I know the tree exists?

Each tree has a page with its species, planting date, GPS point and dated photos. The same records sit in a public register anyone can search.

Who plants the trees?

A local partner named on each project page: a community forest group, a cooperative or a farmers' group. We pay for planting and care, and we check the result.

What if a tree dies?

It is logged on the tree page. Policy to decide: replace within 12 months from a reserve.

Where does my money go?

The split is on the transparency page. The numbers there are placeholders until real costs are known.

Can I visit my tree?

Not promised. Some sites may offer guided visits later. Ask us before you travel.
