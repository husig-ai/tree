Claims kit

Rules on green claims are tightening, so we give you the words that hold up. Copy to be checked by a lawyer before launch.

You can say

  • "We funded the planting of 1,000 trees in Nepal."
  • "Every tree is on a public register with a photo and a position."
  • "We check the trees at 3 and 12 months and publish the result."

Do not say

  • "Carbon neutral" or "climate neutral".
  • "This offsets your trip" or any claim about tonnes of CO2.
  • "These trees will last forever."
