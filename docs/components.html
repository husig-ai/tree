Not copied, on purpose

  • CO2 per tree and tonnes-captured counters (Tree-Nation, Ecologi). Contestable, and possibly inside Nepal's carbon rules. Open legal question.
  • "Offset", "carbon neutral" and "climate positive" wording. EU rules on offset-based claims apply from 2026-09-27.
  • Average-uplift stats on customers (Ecologi), rating scores, award badges, press and customer logos. We have none, and inventing them is the thing being avoided.
  • Real partner names, photos, text and brand styling from any reference site.
  • Blockchain tamper-proofing. Frequent monitoring does the trust work; no source shows a chain adds donor trust.
